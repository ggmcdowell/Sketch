# Mercury's Ashes - Style Guide

## Core Voice

**Tone:** Sparse, industrial, blue-collar noir. Think *Alien* (1979) meets hard sci-fi freighter culture.

**Philosophy:** Show, don't tell. Let physical details carry emotional weight. Trust the reader.

---

## Prose Style

### Sentence Structure
- **Short, punchy sentences for impact**
  - ✅ "It was fuel, nothing more."
  - ✅ "Darkness was cheaper, and he preferred it."
  - ❌ "The darkness was something he preferred because it cost less credits and matched his mood."

- **Occasional longer sentences for rhythm variation**
  - Use commas for pacing, not complexity
  - ✅ "He chewed slowly, deliberately."
  - ✅ "The metal was cold against his feet, a constant reminder of the unforgiving environment outside the station's protective shell."

- **Sentence fragments are acceptable for emphasis**
  - ✅ "Routine, predictable, profitable."
  - ✅ "No room for sentimentality when every kilo counted against payload weight."

### Paragraph Length
- **Vary for pacing:**
  - Single-sentence paragraphs for punch
  - 2-4 sentences for standard
  - Longer paragraphs for world-building dumps (rare)

- **White space matters** - don't overstuff paragraphs

### Adjectives & Adverbs
- **Use sparingly** - choose the right noun/verb instead
  - ✅ "The dull, persistent hum of the station's life support"
  - ✅ "Metallic, bland, efficient."
  - ❌ "The incredibly annoying, frustratingly constant noise"

- **Concrete over abstract**
  - ✅ "Cold, metallic corridor"
  - ❌ "Unwelcoming passage"

- **Sensory details preferred**
  - Temperature: cold, freezing
  - Texture: metallic, synthetic
  - Sound: hum, thrum, chunk
  - Taste: metallic, bland, synthetic

## Character Voice (Lathe)

### Internal Thoughts
- **Pragmatic, not poetic**
  - ✅ "It was fuel, nothing more."
  - ❌ "The ration bar reminded him of the emptiness inside."

- **Self-aware but not self-pitying**
  - ✅ "He knew his father meant well, but the messages felt like a weight."
  - ❌ "He hated himself for avoiding his father's calls."

- **Avoidant of emotion, but emotion leaks through**
  - ✅ "A wave of something akin to guilt washed over him."
  - Shows emotion indirectly - "something akin to guilt" rather than naming it directly

### Dialogue
- **Sparse, functional, no small talk**
  - ✅ "Just trying to earn my keep, Mon."
  - ✅ "She's reliable, Mon. And faster than she looks."

- **Curt responses**
  - ✅ "Maybe next time, Mon," he lied.
  - ✅ "J21." (no elaboration unless pressed)

- **Defends what he cares about (ship, sister)**
  - ✅ "They're not faster, Mon. And my AI is well-tuned. She understands me."

### What Lathe Notices
- **Physical, practical details**
  - Temperature, weight, cost
  - Mechanical systems, repairs, wear patterns
  - Efficiency, fuel, payload

- **Social dynamics observed but not engaged**
  - ✅ "He saw a family saying goodbye to a father leaving for the armada, the wife's forced smile betraying her fear."
  - Watches others' emotions from distance

- **Does NOT notice:**
  - Colors (unless functional)
  - Beauty (unless it's mechanical efficiency)
  - Abstract concepts

## World-Building Approach

### Casual Integration
- **Drop details naturally, never explain**
  - ✅ "He pulled on his worn mag boots, the familiar click of the magnetic soles engaging a small comfort."
  - No explanation of *why* mag boots exist - context makes it clear

- ✅ "With Mercury's heavy metals and proximity to the sun, the odds of having a daughter were about one in twenty-seven."
  - Science-based world detail, stated as fact

### Industrial Atmosphere
- **Everything is worn, repaired, practical**
  - "Patchwork of repairs and modifications"
  - "Standard-issue container"
  - "Rental flight suit"

- **Corporate/blue-collar terminology**
  - "Quota"
  - "Payload weight"
  - "Shipping manifests"
  - "Nav data on a hard drive"

### Technology Presentation
- **Utilitarian, not flashy**
  - ✅ "Compad" (not holographic display)
  - ✅ "Hard drive" (physical media)
  - ✅ "Mag boots" (simple, functional)

- **AI is tool, not character (yet)**
  - ✅ "Irene, it's me. Open up."
  - Brief, functional interaction

## Pacing & Structure

### Scene Openings
- **Start in media res or with physical action**
  - ✅ "Lathe Anderson awoke before his alarm, a phantom vibration still echoing in his skull."
  - ❌ "Lathe had always been an early riser because..."

### Scene Transitions
- **Time/location jumps are clean**
  - ✅ "He left the housing unit, stepping into the cold, metallic corridor."
  - ✅ "He rode another open lift up to the primary dock."

- **Use physical movement to transition**
  - Walking, riding lifts, entering rooms

### Rhythm
- **Action → Reflection → Action**
  - Show him doing something
  - Brief internal thought
  - Back to action

- **Example pattern:**
  - "He ate a ration bar in the dark." (action)
  - "Metallic, bland, efficient. It was fuel, nothing more." (reflection)
  - "He scanned his compad while eating." (action)

---

## Dialogue Tags

### Minimal Tags
- **Default to "said" or no tag at all**
  - ✅ "Lathe, good to see you. Still hauling iron, I see." Mon's voice was gruff, but a hint of amusement played on his lips.
  - ✅ "Just trying to earn my keep, Mon," Lathe replied, offering a curt smile.

- **Action beats over tags**
  - ✅ Lathe cut him with a look.
  - ✅ Mon backpedaled.

### Attribution Placement
- **Usually after dialogue, not before**
  - ✅ "It's faster," Lathe stated.
  - ❌ Lathe stated, "It's faster."

## Sensory Palette

### Dominant Sensations
1. **Cold** - constant, oppressive
2. **Metallic** - taste, smell, texture, sound
3. **Hum/Thrum** - mechanical background noise
4. **Darkness** - preferred, cheap, matches mood
5. **Weight** - physical and metaphorical

### Avoid
- Warmth (rare on Mercury stations)
- Organic smells (unless synthetic)
- Bright colors
- Comfort

---

## Emotional Beats

### Subtlety Over Drama
- **Imply emotion through action**
  - ✅ "He marked the message as read, intending to reply later. Much later."
  - Shows avoidance without stating "he felt guilty"

- ✅ "Lathe lowered his shoulders."
  - Physical release of tension

- ✅ "He took a deep breath of planet air and stepped inside."
  - Moment of transition, savoring something simple

### Acceptable Emotional Moments
- Brief vulnerability when alone
- Pride in his ship
- Protectiveness of sister
- Loyalty to routines

### Avoid
- Melodrama
- Sentimentality
- Overthinking
- Angst

## World-Building Details to Maintain

### Mercury Culture
- Heavy metal environment affects birth ratios (1 in 27 female)
- "Ice Bowl" - northern pole settlement, retired/family area
- Industrial focus: iron mining, refinement
- Blue-collar work ethic
- Proximity to sun is factor in daily life

### Technology Level
- AI companions common (Irene)
- Mag boots standard
- Compads (personal devices)
- Hard drives for secure data
- Cryo-sleep for long transits
- Rental equipment (flight suits)

### Social Structure
- "Armada-eligible" is status goal
- Families rare on Mercury (harsh conditions)
- Freighter pilots are working class
- Pirates exist (mentioned as threat)
- Corporate dominance (Iron and Sons)

### Ship Details (*Long Walk*)
- Family heirloom (father's ship)
- 40 stories tall when docked vertically
- Patchwork of repairs
- Long cargo forks for bulk containers
- Small pilot section
- Notable scar from father's story (asteroid belt)
- AI named Irene

## LitRPG Integration Style

### Principles for Adding Game Elements

**1. Utilitarian Presentation**
- MERIT system should feel like industrial safety equipment
- Blue boxes are HUD readouts, not magical
- Stats are performance metrics, like engine diagnostics

**2. Minimal Intrusion**
- Don't interrupt flow with stat dumps
- Weave numbers into existing observations
- Example: ✅ "His reflexes were rated 14 by the MERIT implant—freighter standard, nothing special."

**3. Match the Tone**
- ✅ "The implant chimed. Routine preflight check complete."
- ❌ "✨ ACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED: PERFECT DOCKING! ✨"

**4. Blue Box Format (When Used)**
```
╔════════════════════════════════════════╗
║ PREFLIGHT STATUS                       ║
║ Fuel: 94% | Cargo: 8,247 tons          ║
║ Route: J21 (high-risk corridor)        ║
║ Pilot Status: Nominal                  ║
╚════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
- Clean, monospaced, industrial
- Information-dense, no fluff
- Appears as needed, not constantly

**5. Skill Names**
- Technical, not fantasy
- ✅ "Precision Docking"
- ✅ "Emergency Vent"
- ❌ "Dragon Strike Maneuver"

**6. Progression Language**
- ✅ "Performance optimization unlocked"
- ✅ "System adaptation: +2 RFX"
- ❌ "LEVEL UP! +5 STATS!"

### Where to Add LitRPG Elements (Chapter 1)

**Early introduction (subtle):**
- "The MERIT implant woke him before the alarm, a phantom vibration in his skull."
- Establishes system exists, matches his "phantom vibration" line

**During compad check:**
- Brief stat readout in corner of screen (fuel efficiency rating, pilot hours)

**Pre-flight routine:**
- System running diagnostics (existing in background)
- ✅ "Irene ran the preflight sequence. Green across the board."

**NOT in Chapter 1:**
- Combat skills (no combat yet)
- Level-up notifications (too early)
- XP tracking (introduce after inciting incident)

## Chapter 1 Specific Goals

### What This Chapter Must Do
1. Establish Lathe's routine, isolation, competence
2. Show his world (Mercury station, freighter culture)
3. Introduce key relationships (father, sister, Mon, Irene)
4. Plant seeds of future (armada dreams, "three more runs")
5. Create atmosphere of normalcy before everything breaks

### Ending Note
- ✅ "Just three more runs, and then his life could really begin."
- Perfect foreshadowing - life *will* change, but not how he expects
- Adds tragic irony when Mercury is destroyed

## Revision Checklist

When editing, ask:
- [ ] Is every word necessary?
- [ ] Are sentences varied in length?
- [ ] Does dialogue sound functional, not literary?
- [ ] Are emotions shown through action/physical detail?
- [ ] Is technology presented as mundane tools?
- [ ] Does pacing move (action → brief reflection → action)?
- [ ] Are world details dropped casually, never explained?
- [ ] Does it feel cold, metallic, industrial?

## Examples of Voice

### Opening Strength
✅ "Lathe Anderson awoke before his alarm, a phantom vibration still echoing in his skull from years of programmed routine."
- Strong sensory detail
- Implies character history (years of routine)
- "Phantom vibration" - precise, evocative

### Emotional Restraint
✅ "A wave of something akin to guilt washed over him."
- Doesn't name the emotion directly
- "Something akin to" shows Lathe analyzing his own feelings
- Matches his avoidant personality

### World-Building Economy
✅ "He pulled on his worn mag boots, the familiar click of the magnetic soles engaging a small comfort."
- Establishes tech (mag boots)
- Shows wear (worn, familiar)
- Hints at emotional state (small comfort)
- All in one sentence

### Dialogue Efficiency
✅ "J21."
✅ "It's faster."
- Lathe doesn't elaborate unless challenged
- Forces other characters to ask questions
- Shows personality through brevity
