# Character Profiles - Mercury's Ashes

## Main Characters

### Lathe (Protagonist)
**Full Name:** Lathe (Mercurian custom: single names)
**Age:** 28
**Background:** Freighter pilot, Mercury native
**Ship:** *Dancer's Reprieve* → *Kess's Knife* (renamed after sister)

**Physical Description:**
- Tall (1.9m), lean build (Mercurian low-gravity adaptation)
- Dark skin, close-cropped hair
- Calm, precise movements (pilot's grace)
- Scarring on hands from industrial work

**Personality (Start):**
- Methodical, patient
- Finds beauty in orbital mechanics
- Loves his sister Kess deeply
- Apolitical, just wants to fly

**Personality (End - Chapter 10):**
- Hardened, ruthless
- Purpose-driven (revenge/remembrance)
- Brilliant tactician
- Morally compromised but self-aware

**MERIT Stats (Chapter 1):**
- Level: 1
- RFX: 14, CMP: 12, SPR: 16, ENG: 6, CNV: 8

**MERIT Stats (Chapter 10):**
- Level: 15
- RFX: 42, CMP: 32, SPR: 48, ENG: 40, CNV: 65

**Key Relationships:**
- Kess (sister, deceased) - motivation
- Kara Vynn (mentor) - surrogate family
- Commander Vess (leader) - respect but questions
- Jorin (squadmate, KIA) - friend, loss hardens him

**Character Arc:**
Civilian → Survivor → Fighter → Engineer → Zealot

---

### Kess (Deceased, Appears in Flashbacks)
**Full Name:** Kess
**Age:** 22 (at death)
**Background:** Geology researcher, Caloris Basin colony

**Physical Description:**
- Shorter than Lathe (1.7m)
- Similar features (siblings)
- Always smiling in memories
- Wore geology scanner goggles habitually

**Personality:**
- Brilliant, curious, optimistic
- Loved Mercury's harsh beauty
- Pestered Lathe to visit more often
- Believed in science solving problems

**Role in Story:**
- Inciting incident (her death drives Lathe)
- Appears in flashbacks, dreams
- Her MERIT core may be salvaged (Kess's Namesake Core)
- Symbol of innocence lost

**Last Words (Recording):**
"Hey big brother, when are you visiting? I found the most amazing mineral formations in sector 7. You'd love the symmetry..."

---

### Commander Vess (Resistance Leader)
**Full Name:** Vess Daemon (callsign: Daemon)
**Age:** 54
**Background:** Mercury traffic control chief, ex-UNE Navy

**Physical Description:**
- Average height, stocky build
- Gray hair, burn scars on left side (pre-Caloris)
- Mechanical left hand (industrial accident)
- Intense gaze, rarely smiles

**Personality:**
- Pragmatic strategist
- Charismatic when needed
- Willing to make hard calls
- Believes the ends justify means

**Background:**
- Served in UNE Navy for 12 years
- Left over colonial exploitation issues
- Ran Mercury orbital traffic (best job in system)
- Lost husband and daughter in Caloris Event

**Leadership Style:**
- Delegates to experts (trusts Lathe's tactical sense)
- Ruthless about acceptable losses
- "We can't win clean, so we win dirty"

**MERIT Stats (Estimated):**
- Level: 18 (veteran)
- Focus: Command & Control specialization
- High CNV (75+), moderate combat skills

**Role in Story:**
- Recruits Lathe
- Provides strategic direction
- Moral foil (shows what Lathe could become)
- Survives to Chapter 10 (series character)

---

### Kara Vynn (Master Engineer)
**Full Name:** Kara Vynn
**Age:** 61
**Background:** Shipwright, ran Mercury's orbital drydock

**Physical Description:**
- Short (1.6m), powerful build
- Prosthetic right arm (industrial grade, tool-integrated)
- Silver hair in practical bun
- Grease-stained clothes permanently

**Personality:**
- Gruff exterior, kind heart
- "Ships don't lie, people do"
- Perfectionist about her work
- Adopts strays (including Lathe)

**Background:**
- 40+ years building ships
- Lost son (age 19) in Caloris Event
- Salvaged her entire drydock, joined Resistance
- Builds the fleet that attacks Earth

**Teaching Style:**
- Hands-on: "I show once, you do twice"
- Demanding but fair
- Rewards innovation, punishes sloppiness
- Sees potential in Lathe's spatial reasoning

**MERIT Stats (Estimated):**
- Level: 16 (non-combat specialist)
- ENG: 75+ (off the standard scale, master craftsman)
- Low CNV (38) - motivated by remembrance, not revenge

**Role in Story:**
- Mentor for Engineering subclass
- Surrogate mother figure
- Voice of conscience (occasionally)
- Provides Kess's Namesake Core (Chapter 6)

**Key Quote:**
"Every ship I build is a prayer. Every weapon, a name. They'll remember us, kid. Even if we have to carve it into their hulls."

---

### Jorin (Squadmate, KIA)
**Full Name:** Jorin Torch (callsign: Torch)
**Age:** 24
**Background:** Hot-shot pilot, Mars native (volunteered for Resistance)

**Physical Description:**
- Young, cocky demeanor
- Red hair (rare), freckles
- Martian tall (2.0m)
- Always grinning

**Personality:**
- Brash, confident, skilled
- Adrenaline junkie
- Hides insecurity with bravado
- Genuinely brave

**Background:**
- Martian racing circuit champion
- Joined Resistance for "excitement" (claims)
- Actually: Moral outrage at Mercury's destruction
- Becomes Lathe's wingman and friend

**MERIT Stats (Chapter 8):**
- Level: 11
- High RFX (40), low CMP (22) - reckless
- Ace pilot, poor tactician

**Death:**
- Chapter 8: First Battle of Earth
- Reactor breach during frigate engagement
- Dies on comms, last words: "Worth it"
- Body unrecoverable (vaporized)

**Impact on Lathe:**
- First personal loss since Kess
- Hardens Lathe's resolve
- CNV +10 from grief
- Lathe names a maneuver after him later

---

### Mira (Resistance Pilot)
**Full Name:** Mira Cold (callsign: Ice)
**Age:** 35
**Background:** Former mining ship pilot, Mercury native

**Physical Description:**
- Pale skin (albinism, adapted for underground life)
- White hair, red eyes
- Thin, angular features
- Moves like a predator

**Personality:**
- Stone-cold killer
- Minimal emotion (trauma response)
- Brilliant tactician
- "I don't hate them. I just kill them."

**Background:**
- Lost entire family (husband, 3 children) in Caloris
- Shut down emotionally to cope
- Joined Resistance day 1
- Highest kill count in fleet (40+ confirmed)

**MERIT Stats (Chapter 10):**
- Level: 17
- Maxed RFX (50), very high CNV (80)
- Specialization: Assassin (ambush tactics)

**Role in Story:**
- Foil to Lathe (shows extreme of his path)
- Occasionally works with his squad
- Survives to Chapter 10 (series character)
- Represents "what if Lathe loses all humanity"

---

### Old Dane (Veteran Pilot)
**Full Name:** Dane Ashen
**Age:** 68
**Background:** Pre-war veteran, multiple conflicts

**Physical Description:**
- Weathered, scarred
- Gray beard, missing left eye (replaced with basic prosthetic)
- Slow, deliberate movements
- Stronger than he looks

**Personality:**
- Philosophical, tired
- Fights because "someone has to"
- Protective of younger pilots
- Knows war's true cost

**Background:**
- Fought in Belt Independence Skirmish (2378)
- Retired to Mercury, lost daughter in Caloris
- "I'm too old for this. But here I am."

**MERIT Stats (Chapter 10):**
- Level: 14 (peak was higher, age degradation)
- Balanced stats, decades of experience
- CNV: 45 (measured, not fanatic)

**Role in Story:**
- Wisdom figure
- Provides historical context
- Survives battles younger pilots don't (experience)
- Voice of weariness with war

**Key Quote:**
"I've seen three wars, kid. We never win. We just stop losing for a while."

## Character Growth Arcs

**Lathe:**
- Act 1: Loss → Rage
- Act 2: Skill → Purpose
- Act 3: Mastery → Zealotry

**Kara:**
- Grief channeled into creation
- Finds meaning in building weapons
- Maternal instinct transferred to Lathe

**Vess:**
- Calculated leader becomes true believer
- Sacrifices morality for victory
- Mirror of Lathe's future

**Jorin:**
- Cocky kid → Competent warrior → Martyr
- Death catalyzes Lathe's final transformation

---

This character foundation provides depth, relationships, and clear arcs for the narrative.
