## Weapons Technology

### Lasers

**Types:**
1. **Continuous Wave (CW):** Sustained beam, high heat generation
2. **Pulsed:** Bursts of energy, better heat management
3. **Mining Lasers:** CW, industrial power, not optimized for combat

**Damage Mechanics:**
- Ablation: Vaporizes surface material
- Thermal: Overheats target systems
- Blinding: Destroys sensors (even at non-lethal range)

**Range Effectiveness:**
- < 10 km: Lethal (can cut through armor)
- 10-50 km: Damaging (heats systems, sensors)
- 50-200 km: Sensor disruption only
- > 200 km: Ineffective (beam spread, time for evasion)

**Counterplay:**
- Reflective coating (reduces damage 30-50%)
- Evasion (works at range)
- Point defense (ablate incoming beam with own laser)

---

### Kinetic Weapons

**Railguns:**
- Electromagnetic acceleration
- Muzzle velocity: 5-20 km/s
- Projectile: Solid slug (tungsten, depleted uranium, or improvised)
- Damage: Penetration, kinetic energy transfer
- Advantage: No heat generation (for shooter)
- Drawback: Ammo-limited, dodgeable at long range

**Coilguns:**
- Magnetic coil acceleration
- Slower than railguns (3-8 km/s)
- More efficient (less power per shot)
- Common on civilian ships (mining, construction)

**Cargo-as-Weapon (Resistance Specialty):**
- Launch cargo containers as missiles
- Velocity: 1-5 km/s
- Guidance: Basic RCS, laser link control
- Damage: Mass × velocity (Newtonian!)
- Psychological impact: "They're throwing their *stuff* at us"

---

### Missiles

**Types:**
1. **Guided Missiles:** Self-propelled, tracking systems
2. **Torpedoes:** Long-range, high-Δv, autonomous
3. **Nuclear:** Banned in inner system, but exist

**Performance:**
- Δv: 2,000-10,000 m/s (depends on type)
- Range: 100-50,000 km
- Guidance: IR tracking, radar, laser designation
- Warhead: Explosive, fragmentation, nuclear

**Counterplay:**
- Point defense lasers
- Electronic warfare (jamming, spoofing)
- Evasion (if detected early)

**Usage in Story:**
- UNE uses missiles frequently (standard doctrine)
- Resistance uses sparingly (limited supply)

## Sensor & Electronic Warfare

### Detection Systems

**Infrared (IR):**
- Detects heat signatures
- Range: 100,000 km+ (for hot ships)
- Passive (doesn't reveal your position)
- Hard to fool (thermodynamics is cruel)

**Radar/Lidar:**
- Active ping (reveals your position)
- Range: 50,000 km (depends on power)
- Accurate positioning
- Can be jammed

**Optical:**
- Telescopes, spectroscopy
- Range: Unlimited (if target is bright enough)
- Passive
- Requires line of sight

**Neutrino Detection:**
- Detects fusion reactors
- Range: 1 million km+
- Cannot be blocked
- Expensive, rare (only on capital ships)

---

### Stealth

**Cold Running:**
- Minimize reactor output
- Reduce radiator exposure
- Effect: Reduces detection range 50-90%
- Cost: Limited power (no weapons, minimal life support)

**Reflective Coating:**
- Reduces radar cross-section
- Reflects some laser damage
- Effect: -30% detectability, -20% laser damage
- Cost: Expensive, degrades over time

**Orbital Mechanics Stealth:**
- Use planets/moons for cover
- Drift on ballistic trajectory (no thrust = no signature)
- Effect: Can approach undetected
- Cost: Slow, requires precise planning

**Truth:** True stealth is impossible (radiators must dump heat), only delayed detection

---

### Electronic Warfare

**Jamming:**
- Broadcast noise on enemy frequencies
- Effect: Disrupts targeting, communications
- Range: 10,000 km
- Drawback: Reveals your position

**Decoys:**
- False heat signatures (inflatable radiators)
- Radar reflectors
- Effect: Enemy wastes ammunition
- Limitation: Sophisticated sensors can detect (velocity mismatch)

**Hacking:**
- MERIT systems are networked (vulnerable)
- Can disrupt enemy ship systems
- Rare (requires specialized skills, proximity)
- Lathe doesn't use this (not his specialty)

## Propulsion Deep Dive

### Fusion Torch Drives

**Mechanism:**
- Deuterium-Helium3 fusion
- Exhaust: Superheated plasma
- Thrust: Continuous (limited by fuel)

**Efficiency (Specific Impulse):**
- ISP: 30,000-50,000 seconds
- High ISP = fuel-efficient but lower thrust
- Trade-off: Acceleration vs. Δv reserves

**Heat Generation:**
- Every burn generates waste heat
- Radiators required (vulnerable to damage)
- Combat limit: Thermal capacity, not fuel

---

### Reaction Control Systems (RCS)

**Mechanism:**
- Cold gas thrusters (nitrogen, CO2)
- Used for rotation, fine maneuvering
- Distinct from main drive

**Fuel Budget:**
- 200-500 m/s total Δv (much less than main drive)
- Expended quickly in combat (evasive maneuvers)
- Running out = can't rotate = dead

**Lathe's Advantage:**
- Freighter background = expert RCS use (docking precision)
- *Kess's Knife* has oversized RCS (RCS 10)

## Life Support & Habitat Systems

**Oxygen:**
- Electrolysis (water → O2 + H2)
- Scrubbers (remove CO2)
- Efficiency: 98% (need periodic resupply)

**Water:**
- Recycling (urine, humidity, waste)
- Efficiency: 95% (2-3% loss per cycle)
- Critical resource (also used for radiation shielding)

**Food:**
- Freeze-dried, hydroponic (on large ships)
- Fighters: Ration bars, water (enough for 72 hours)
- Lathe's preference: Martian coffee substitute (tastes like dirt, keeps you awake)

**Radiation Shielding:**
- Water tanks around crew compartment
- Magnetic deflection (energy-intensive)
- Shelter during solar storms (reinforced core)

**G-Forces:**
- Sustained acceleration: Pilot must handle G-stress
- Blackout: High-G turns can cause unconsciousness
- Lathe's G-tolerance: ~7G (Chapter 1), ~10G (Chapter 10)
