# Chapter 1: The Last Run

**Epigraph:** *"Before the fire, there was ice."*

**Level Start:** 1
**Level End:** 1
**Location:** Mercury orbit → Caloris Basin
**Word Count Target:** ~15,000 words

---

## Story Beats

### Opening (Scene 1)
- Lathe piloting *Dancer's Reprieve* on routine cargo run
- MERIT system introduction (natural tutorial through docking sequence)
- Establish character: calm, competent, loves orbital mechanics
- Worldbuilding: Mercury industrial output, Venus destination

### The Call Home (Scene 2)
- Personal beacon from Kess
- Establishes sibling relationship (warm, loving)
- Kess invites Lathe to visit Caloris Basin
- Foreshadowing: Mentions "unusual solar activity" casually

### The Descent (Scene 3)
- Lathe enters Mercurian orbit
- MERIT displays orbital calculations (showcase system)
- Describe Mercury: Harsh beauty, precision required
- Moment of peace before catastrophe

### The Caloris Event (Scene 4)
- Solar flare erupts (visual description: Sun *screams*)
- Magnetic field collapse (sensor data, MERIT alerts)
- Lathe watches from orbit: Surface becomes inferno
- **Physics Detail:** Temperature spike, atmosphere stripped, structural failures
- Personal horror: Realizes Kess was in Caloris Basin

### Aftermath (Scene 5)
- MERIT system reboots from EM pulse
- Blue box: **CNV +10** (Trauma Response unlocking)
- Zero transmissions from surface
- Lathe's world ends in silence

## Technical Details to Include

### Orbital Mechanics
- Hohmann transfer from Mercury to Venus (Lathe's original route)
- Mercury's eccentric orbit (0.21 eccentricity)
- Perihelion vs aphelion delta-v costs
- Lathe's natural talent for trajectory calculation (SPR 16)

### Physics of Destruction
- Solar flare X-147 classification (10^47 magnitude)
- Magnetic channeling (controversial: natural vs induced by Dyson Swarm)
- Atmospheric stripping timeframe: 8 minutes
- Surface temperature spike: 430°C → 1000°C+
- Structural collapse of underground cities (thermal expansion)

### MERIT System Tutorial
- HUD overlay (augmented reality)
- Delta-v budget display
- Heat management (normally not critical for freighters)
- Skill activation (Precision Docking during approach)

## Character Development

### Lathe (Start of Chapter)
- Content with life as freighter pilot
- Apolitical, just wants to fly
- Family-oriented (close with Kess)
- Finds beauty in mathematics of spaceflight

### Lathe (End of Chapter)
- Shattered, grieving
- Questions everything (How? Why? Who?)
- MERIT system shows first signs of radicalization (CNV increase)
- Seed planted: Someone is to blame

---

## Themes

- **Normalcy Shattered:** Life can end in moments
- **Scale of Loss:** 47 million deaths is incomprehensible
- **Helplessness:** Lathe could do nothing but watch
- **Beauty and Horror:** Orbital mechanics are beautiful, destruction is not

---

## Narrative Techniques

- **Contrast:** Peaceful opening vs catastrophic ending
- **Sensory Detail:** Cold vacuum vs burning hell
- **MERIT Integration:** Stats inform emotional state (CNV spike shows trauma)
- **Restraint:** Don't over-explain, trust reader intelligence

---

## Notes for Writing

- Open with action (docking sequence) not exposition
- Let MERIT system emerge naturally through use
- Kess should feel real in brief appearance (readers must care)
- Mercury's destruction: Clinical details (numbers, physics) contrasted with personal horror
- End on silence: Zero transmissions = absolute death
