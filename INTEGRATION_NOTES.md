### Chapter 2+ Guidelines

#### Blue Box Frequency
- **Chapter 1:** 1 box (preflight)
- **Chapter 2:** 2 boxes (trauma response, skill unlock)
- **Chapter 3+:** 2-4 per chapter (combat, progression)

#### When to Show Stats
- **Always:** During significant moments (first kill, level up, new skill)
- **Sometimes:** During technical sequences (docking, combat maneuvers)
- **Never:** During emotional scenes (unless the system is the point)

#### Skill Unlocks
Use **technical names** that match your world:

✅ Good (Industrial/Military):
- Precision Docking
- Emergency Vent
- Rapid Course Correction
- Heat Management Protocol
- Combat Pattern Recognition

❌ Bad (Too game-y):
- Dragon Strike
- Power Overwhelming
- Berserker Rage
- Holy Shield

#### Level-Ups
Keep them **understated**:

```
╔════════════════════════════════════════╗
║ PERFORMANCE THRESHOLD ACHIEVED         ║
║                                        ║
║ Level 1 → 2                            ║
║                                        ║
║ Attribute allocation available         ║
║ +5 points                              ║
║                                        ║
║ [Defer] [Allocate]                     ║
╚════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

Then Lathe's reaction:
```
He selected [Defer]. He'd deal with it later.
He had more important things to think about.
```

Shows the system exists but **Lathe's personality comes first**.
